❓ ID Maker FAQ & User Guide

📖 How to Use the App

  1. Setup Workspace & Projects: Enter your Entity name, then use the top project bar to create new card designs or switch between projects.
  2. Design Layout (Tab 1): Add shapes, text labels, barcodes, QR codes, photo areas, or logos. Double-click any text box to type, highlight words, and press Enter for new lines! Text will automatically resize if it reaches the bottom or right edge.
  3. Canvas Toolbar & Shortcuts: Use the frozen toolbar right above the canvas (Undo, Redo, Cut, Copy, Paste, Delete) or keyboard shortcuts (`Ctrl+Z`, `Ctrl+Y`, `Ctrl+C`, `Ctrl+X`, `Ctrl+V`, `Delete`).
  4. Map Database Fields: Select any text label, barcode, or photo on your card, and change its Data Source to Database in the right-side inspector panel.
  5. Populate Database (Tab 2): Add your employee or member records, upload pictures, and check the boxes for who you want to print.
  6. Generate & Print (Tab 3): Review your generated cards. You can drag photos to center faces or use the zoom slider. Click Print Cards.

💡 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I print dual-sided (front and back) cards?
A: In Tab 1, set Card Format to Dual-Sided (Front & Back). Back design controls will appear, allowing you to add elements to the back canvas.

Q: How do I get my IDP SMART-51 printer to format cards correctly without extra pages?
A: In your browser's print dialog, set Scale to 100% (or Actual Size), set Margins to None, and disable headers/footers.
